Joining Atlassian as a Frontend Software Engineer, contributing to world-class collaboration tools that help teams unleash their potential.

## About Atlassian

Atlassian is a global leader in team collaboration software, with products used by millions of users across over 200,000 organisations worldwide. The company is renowned for its innovative approach to teamwork and its unique company culture built around strong values.

### Products

- **Jira** — Industry-leading project tracking and issue management
- **Confluence** — Team workspace for knowledge sharing and collaboration
- **Trello** — Visual project management with flexible boards
- **Bitbucket** — Git-based code collaboration and CI/CD

As a Frontend Software Engineer at Atlassian, I'm working on building and maintaining the user interfaces that millions of people interact with daily. The role involves:

- Building accessible, performant frontend experiences
- Collaborating with cross-functional teams across design, product, and engineering
- Contributing to Atlassian's design system and shared component libraries
- Championing frontend best practices and modern development techniques

Atlassian's values-driven culture and commitment to building products that genuinely help teams work better together aligned perfectly with my passion for creating impactful software.
