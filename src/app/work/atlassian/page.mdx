Atlassian is a global leader in team collaboration software, with products used by millions of users across over 200,000 organisations worldwide. The company is renowned for its innovative approach to teamwork and its unique company culture built around strong values.

### Products

- **Jira** — Industry-leading project tracking and issue management
- **Confluence** — Team workspace for knowledge sharing and collaboration
- **Rovo** — AI-powered teamwork companion with intelligent search and chat
- **Trello** — Visual project management with flexible boards
- **Bitbucket** — Git-based code collaboration and CI/CD
